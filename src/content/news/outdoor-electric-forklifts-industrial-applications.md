body:
  en:
    - Electric forklifts are no longer limited to simple indoor tasks, but
      outdoor suitability must be established from the site conditions. Quiet
      operation and no tailpipe emissions can be useful near offices, retail
      areas, covered production, and loading docks.
    - An outdoor route may include concrete, uneven pavement, ramps, rain
      exposure, changing temperatures, and longer travel distances. The truck
      needs stable handling, suitable ground clearance, correct tyres, and a
      battery plan that reflects the real operating hours.
    - Confirm capacity, load center, tyre type, chassis protection, ground
      clearance, mast, lighting, weather exposure, ramp grade, battery capacity,
      charger location, and attachment. Electric power does not make every model
      suitable for every yard or surface.
    - Before quotation, provide route photos, surface details, rain or dust
      exposure, ramp information, load profile, lift height, daily hours, shift
      count, parking, charging, and indoor-outdoor transitions. Compare the data
      with diesel or LPG alternatives where conditions are demanding.
    - For export orders, confirm charging standards, weather-related operating
      guidance, manuals, spare parts, service intervals, training, and local
      support. WEGO can compare electric and internal-combustion options for a
      documented indoor-outdoor application.
