  en:
    - Lithium-ion forklifts are increasingly considered by warehouses that want quieter, cleaner, and more flexible material handling. The purchase decision should cover the complete truck, battery, charger, and service plan rather than focusing only on the battery label or initial price.
    - In a distribution center, an electric forklift may move pallets between receiving, storage, picking, and dispatch throughout the day. Opportunity charging during planned breaks can support availability, but the site still needs safe charging rules, suitable power, clear traffic routes, and trained operators.
    - Confirm rated capacity at the required lift height, load center, mast visibility, tyre type, attachment, battery capacity, charger input, and expected shift pattern. Indoor use benefits from zero tailpipe emissions, while floor quality, ramps, temperature, and travel distance still affect the practical configuration.
    - Before quotation, provide average and maximum load, pallet size, lift height, aisle width, daily hours, number of shifts, charging windows, electrical supply, and indoor conditions. Also state whether the fleet needs common chargers, spare batteries, telematics, or a phased replacement plan.
    - For export delivery, agree on charger compatibility, documentation language, commissioning, training, preventive maintenance, spare parts, and technical support. WEGO can use the application data to recommend a lithium electric forklift package that supports reliable long-term warehouse work.
